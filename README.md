# Blogr for Claude

blogr plans, writes and publishes SEO content for your website. This plugin connects Claude to your blogr account so you can check on that work from a conversation.

## Use it

Add the plugin, then connect the blogr connector from the plugin's Connectors tab and sign in with your blogr account. Then ask things like:

- "How did my content do this week?" gives a review of Search Console performance, article movement, and anything that needs your attention.
- "What's scheduled next, and what's waiting for my approval?"
- "Which silos in my topical map are still thin?"
- "Approve the three best titles in my approval tray."
- "Add an article about Laravel queues in production for next week."
- "Which articles slipped in Google? Rewrite their meta descriptions."
- "Turn my latest article into a LinkedIn post and a newsletter intro."

Run `/blogr:review` in Cowork or Claude Code for the weekly review.

The connector sees the same websites you do in blogr, with your team role. It needs an active blogr plan or trial on the website's owner account.

## Data

Claude reads your websites, articles, topical map, Search Console performance and SEO opportunities from your blogr account through blogr.ai/mcp. It can also change your content plan when you ask: approve, decline, add and reschedule titles, and edit article drafts. It does not publish, delete, or start writing an article early; approved titles are written on their scheduled day within your plan's monthly allowance. Your team role in blogr decides what Claude may change. blogr logs which tools were called (not your conversation) for 90 days.
